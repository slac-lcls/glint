Recorded replay — not a live beamline, and not a simulation. Every frame pushed through this monitor is a peak list from the 480-frame extension of the cxidb-17 sparse lysozyme run (CXIDB entry 17, CC0) — the paper's primary streaming set, four times the published 120-frame subset, and the same run and the same crystals: 480 buys statistical power, not generality. One protein. Nothing injected, nothing planted, no second species, no drift.

Where the peaks come from. They are CrystFEL 0.12.0 peakfinder8 peaks, read out of the blind indexamajig stream for these 480 images and converted to reciprocal-space q-vectors through the package's own bridge, glint.geom.read_crystfel_peaks + peaks_to_q — not a re-derivation, so panel membership, coffset and the wavelength convention cannot drift. Following the published 120-frame benchmark, one fixed wavelength (1.322216 Å, 9377.00 eV — frame 0's photon energy) is used for every frame rather than the per-shot SASE energy; that is the convention the published q lists were built under, and it is worth a few frames per arm, so it is stated rather than assumed. No pixels are replayed and no peak finder runs here — the driver is fed q-vectors, so no peak-finding lane is claimed.

The first 120 of this list are not the published 120-frame subset. Sliced to its first 120 frames this input gives 76 / 79 at the strict bar where the published subset gives 73 / 78: the rebuilt lists keep peaks in the last fractional pixel of each panel that the pre-fix geometry code dropped. Different peak lists, expected, not a discrepancy — but it is why the number on this page should be quoted with its input named.

The indexer is the shipped glint.stream_driver.StreamDriver at commit 2d6eacb53a33, cold-started with no cell (Mc=None), at its constructor defaults plus the two published opt-ins warmup_rescue=True and adaptive_relock=True: B=20, dmin=2.0, tol=0.002, warmup_nbest=3, live accept gate min_inlier_frac=0.15, min_inliers=10. OFF: retry cascade, live geometry refinement, second-lattice detection, alias gate, lock probe, miss-buffer rescue. Panels those options would have filled are removed rather than left idle, which is why there is no geometry box, no injected-QA row and no spurious meter on this build.

Consensus locked the cell after 5 frames on 4 votes; the 5 frames spent on discovery were re-indexed against it and 3 of them cleared the strict bar. The driver accepted 451 of 480 at its own live gate and refused 24. Scored at the paper's strict research bar (≥25% of spots and ≥10 reflections, correct lattice), the run indexes 331/480 (69.0%) — 328 post-lock plus the 3 rescued. That is the high end of the range the paper quotes for the streaming driver on this set, “67-69% of the 480-frame set” (323–331 of 480): the low end is the same driver with warmup_rescue and adaptive_relock both off, so the two options enabled here are published arms, not tuned ones. The offline known-cell reference on the same frames and the same gate is 357/480 (74.4%).

What 480 frames show that 120 cannot. Across 120 frames of this same list the watchdog fires zero times. Across these 480 it performs 6 individual rescues — frames the batch pass missed, blind-solved again one at a time and accepted after all — and one re-lock, at frame 364. Read the re-lock honestly: it does not replace the locked cell, it adds a second cell to the active set, and that added cell is not the lysozyme lattice (87.5, 87.6, 109.5, 69.3, 72.7, 100.7) — it is a cell voted by frames the active set could not explain. Every number on this page is scored against the first locked cell only, so the re-lock adds nothing to the count; it is on screen because it is what the mechanism did.

Recorded on NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB (sdfampere042), cupy 12.3.0, torch 2.1.0.post302, numpy 1.26.3; 10.5 s of wall time for the whole replay. Playback is looped and the frame rate is a display choice; every counter, the recovered cell, the lock frame, the ring occupancy, the rescues and the re-lock are read out of that one recorded run. The cell is printed as measured and is not re-jittered for the animation.
